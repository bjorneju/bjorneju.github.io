## Research Identity and Core Focus

Bjørn's central research question is: *How can we objectively measure whether a person or system is conscious?* He approaches this from multiple angles — theoretical, computational, empirical, and clinical. His work sits at the intersection of:

- **Consciousness science** — both empirical (what the brain does during conscious and unconscious states) and theoretical (what consciousness fundamentally is)
- **Clinical neuroscience** — applying consciousness measures in anesthesia monitoring and palliative care
- **Computational neuroscience** — modeling, simulating, and mathematically formalizing consciousness

His intellectual background is unusually broad: physics → machine learning → theoretical neuroscience → empirical EEG research → clinical translation → science communication. This gives him fluency across rigorous mathematical formalism, experimental design, and clinical application.
